Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Obsidian GSync is a personal OAuth app used by a single person (its owner) to sync their own Obsidian notes with a folder in their own Google Drive.

Data accessed

When the owner signs in with Google, the app requests access to Google Drive so the Obsidian GSync plugin can read, create, update and delete the Markdown note files in the owner's sync folder.

How data is used

Data is used only to keep the owner's notes in sync between Obsidian and Google Drive. The app runs no server. Tokens and notes stay on the owner's devices and in the owner's Google account.

Sharing and storage

We do not collect, sell, share or transfer Google user data to any third party, and we do not use it for advertising or to train AI models. This website is static and stores no data.

Deletion

The owner can revoke access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions and delete the sync folder in Google Drive.
